01 · The idea

The friend who always has the reservation.

La Güera’s Guide is Ashley’s Mexico City: the places she actually sends her friends to, on a map, in her words. It is confident, dry, a little bitchy, never mean, never cutesy.

What it is
A guide of places Ashley picked. Not a directory. Not a review site.
How it sounds
First person. Plain, specific, short. One real detail beats three adjectives.
How it looks
Oxblood and pink, checkerboard, a Didot headline, her handwriting. Natural photos, no filter.

06 · Patterns, shape, space

Checkerboards, arches, and air.

Checker · the signature. Vino 800 / Rosa 300, 48px squares
Storia · pink, powder blue, wine
Gingham · tomate and olive on pink
Stripes · Rosa 200 / tomate
Carpet · fine pink lines on tomate

The checker is the brand’s one signature pattern: use it behind the logo, on covers and behind place cards with no photo. The others are occasional accents. One pattern per layout, never two.

Shape

Buttons and tags are pills. The arch (round top, flat bottom) is for photos and frames. Cards 8–14 px radius; photos 4 px.

Depth

Soft shadow for cards, float shadow for dialogs and the map card. Shadows are always wine-tinted, never grey or black. Lines are 1.5 px.

Spacing scale

4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96. Generous white space: when unsure, add more. Page content stays within 1280 px.

09 · Photos

As she shot them, cropped 4:5.

No filter, no color grade, no grain, no vignette. Photos go in the app and on Instagram as Ashley shot them. The only edit is the crop to 4:5, output 1080×1350. (The old photo-grade look was retired on 2026-11-06.)

  • Photos are Ashley’s, or ones she pulls from a venue and sends. Credit on every photo: “Photo: La Güera” or the venue’s name.
  • Originals beat screenshots. Crop out app bars.
  • No stock. No agent scraping Instagram.
  • For social, keep people out of frame.
  • Name a dish only if Ashley confirmed it.

10 · Voice

Say the one real thing.

“By far my favorite Italian in town… I’m ordering the prosciutto before I even sit down. Save room for the tiramisu.”
la güera · on Lalo
“Amazing seafood and the biggest steak I’ve seen in Mexico City.”
la güera · on El Silverio
First person (“my favorite”). Plain, specific, short. What to order, when to go, one tip. Only claims that are researched or that Ashley said herself. Sign with xo, la güera only on her own words.
No “we” or “our team”. No adjective stacks. No invented dishes or details. No planner talk (“one fixed thing”, “anchor”, “flexible block”, “47 tabs”). No hype (“best ever”), no emoji, no “coming soon”.

11 · Instagram

@laguerasguide, draft only.

The account is Ashley’s. Agents only draft; Ashley approves and posts. Profile picture: the wordmark on oxblood or on pale pink. She is choosing.

  • Carousels: full-bleed photos, one small account label, name and colonia on the cover, a save line and the address on the last slide. 1080×1350.
  • Captions are Ashley’s words, or her approved Pick text.
  • Less, better: start with a few posts a week, not a daily grind.
  • No bought followers, pods, auto-DMs, or tools that ask for her password.
Full rules: the private repo’s social/SOCIAL_GUIDELINES.md. The first carousel to copy is the Lalo one.

12 · Social sizes

Sizes, safe areas, and the recipe.

FEED · 4:5
1080 × 1350. Posts and carousels (the standard).
STORY · 9:16
1080 × 1920. Stories, reels, covers. Keep text away from the top and bottom ~250 px.
PROFILE
1:1
1080 × 1080, shown as a circle. Keep the logo well inside.
LINK PREVIEW
1200 × 630. For link previews (once the domain exists).

Carousel recipe: cover is a full-bleed photo with the venue name and one line in Ashley’s words. Slides 2–6 are full-bleed photos, one small label, venue and colonia. The last slide is a short save line and the address. Name and colonia on the cover only; no “swipe” prompts. Captions are Ashley’s words or her approved Pick text.

The 9:16, 1200×630 and safe-area numbers are general Instagram and web conventions, not choices Ashley made. 4:5 and 1080×1080 are the ones the app and posts already use.
