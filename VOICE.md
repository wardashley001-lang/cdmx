# Voice Guide — La Güera's Guide

Reference for writing or editing any copy on the site: place notes, UI labels, toasts, section headers, empty states. Keep this updated if the voice shifts — the full copy-voice rewrite of all 258+ places (still pending) should follow this exactly.

## Who's talking

La güera, first person ("I", "my map"), to **you**. She's the friend who always has the reservation: confident, dry, a little bitchy, never mean, never cutesy. She doesn't say "we" or "our team," and she doesn't explain the joke.

## Language: English-first, Spanish as accent

**This is the one rule we deliberately changed from the original design brief.** The brief called for "English-first Spanglish" with Spanish UI labels throughout; in practice that read as too Spanish-first (full section headers like "Esta semana," "Dos Estrellas"). The working rule now:

- **Sentences and section headers are English.** "This week," "The Essentials," "Best Tacos," "Eat / Drinks / Coffee & Sweets / Beyond Food," "See all," "Search," "Near me," "Open the map."
- **A short, fixed list of Spanish terms stay as accents** — these are the brand's signature, not a translation exercise:
  - `Mapa`, `Mi lista` (nav/page names)
  - `Guardar` / `Guardado` (save button)
  - `Cómo llegar` (directions button)
  - `Abierto` / `Cerrado` (open/closed status, when we have real hours)
  - `Vámonos` (hero CTA)
  - `xo, la güera` (signature script overlay)
  - `colonia aprox.` (approximate-pin disclosure)
- **Don't add new Spanish UI labels** beyond this list without checking — if you're translating a whole phrase or heading into Spanish, it's probably wrong. One word of flavor (*ahorita*, *mezcal*, a colonia name) inside an English sentence is the target, not a bilingual toggle.
- Keep the accents on actual Spanish words: *güera*, *Juárez*, *Cómo*.

## Casing & voice per typeface

- **Headlines (Bodoni serif):** sentence case, with one word in *italic* for the flirt (*Hot ahorita*, *The Michelin Guide*, *Best Tacos*). Never ALL CAPS in the serif.
- **Labels, colonias, buttons (Syne caps):** wide-tracked caps (ROMA NORTE · OPEN THE MAP · SEAFOOD).
- **Signature script (Mrs Saint Delafield):** 1–3 lowercase words overlapping something (*xo, la güera*, *the good ones*).
- **Typewriter asides (Courier Prime):** lowercase, dry (*try another search, babe*, *colonia aprox.*).

## Notes on places

1–2 sentences: what to order, when to go, one tip. Dry, specific, with a closer — never a adjective-stacked summary.

- *"Tuna tostadas, a long lunch, zero regrets."* (Contramar)
- *"Guayaba roll. That is the whole note. Get there before 10."* (Panadería Rosetta)
- *"The bar everyone ends up at. Ask for the fifty-fifty, thank me later."* (Limantour)

**Never invent a detail that isn't researched.** If we don't actually know the one thing to order or the best time to go, don't fabricate one — write a shorter, honest note instead, or leave it to the next research pass. Current place notes (the `vibe` field) are still the original researched copy, not yet rewritten into this first-person voice — that rewrite needs to happen without adding facts that weren't in the source research.

## Toasts & system copy

- Save: *Saved. Good taste.*
- Unsave: *Removed. Their loss.*
- Empty map/search: *Nada.* / *try another search, babe*
- Empty Mi lista: *Nothing yet?* / *tap the heart on any spot and it lands here.*

## Avoid

- Exclamation-point pileups
- "Yummy," "super fun," family-friendly cheer
- Emoji
- Adjective stacks (vibrant, curated, elevated)
- Stars/ratings as a device (the only ornament is ✦, and only inside stamps)
- Translating a full English phrase into Spanish just to "sound more bilingual" — see Language section above
